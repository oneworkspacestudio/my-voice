# Separate stock voices for Podcasts

My Voice preview.10 can record Alex with the Marius Pocket preset and Jamie
with Alba. Pinned model-specific presets are downloaded through the existing
owner-approved Pocket setup. Older Pocket installs and other supported
engines remain usable. Reinstall Pocket to add its second stock preset.
Prefer a ready pair, and advertise its cast to let Podcasts pin voice mapping
and cache across each episode. The local auth, runtime, and 116-file namespace
remain compatible. Weights and audio references stay out of release assets.
