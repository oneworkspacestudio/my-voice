existing managed My Voice process and supervised CPU runtime. Direct loopback
endpoints reject forwarded/browser requests. Store only a hash of the grant.

mono PCM. Existing owner APIs retain model installation and private data access.

three signed-out Podcasts WAVs and one previously reviewed script, with
synthetic research and writing. Proxy denial, revocation, Go/vet and nineteen
browser regressions passed. Publish via the existing 116-file Voice update
